I'll create supporting documents for a world built on the Three-Sphere Method and Three-Layer Approach. The scenario is set in a futuristic city where advanced neurotechnology has both utopian and dystopian applications.

World Document: Neo-Elysia, 2342 AD

Surface Layer: Neo-Elysia is a gleaming metropolis of biomorphic architecture and seamless augmented reality overlays. Citizens wear neural interfaces called "Synapse Weaves" that enhance cognition, regulate emotions, and provide instant knowledge access. The city boasts zero crime rates, perfect health outcomes, and unprecedented creative collaboration through "Mind-Link" technology.

Shadow Layer: Beneath the polished surface, three factions battle for control:

    Cognition Corp (CogCorp)
        Public role: Manufacturer of Synapse Weaves
        Shadow role: Harvests neural data from millions, selling behavioral prediction models
        Power: Control over information flow and emotional manipulation

    The Unplugged
        Public role: Naturalist wellness movement
        Synapse Weave alternatives for those who experience adverse effects or ethical objections
        Power: Growing counter-narrative, exploits system vulnerabilities
